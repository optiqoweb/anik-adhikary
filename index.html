About

I founded Graphic Design Ocean to bring consistent, thoughtful design to clients who don't usually get easy access to it — from small businesses to personal brands.

Alongside studio work, I contribute design and illustration to two Bangladeshi dailies, National Daily Abhaynagar and The Daily Anulipi, and spend a good part of my week working with AI as a creative tool — as a virtual artist and as a prompt writer.

I also write stories, speak on motivation and creative confidence, and keep a running photography habit — most of it unrelated to client work, all of it feeding back into it.
